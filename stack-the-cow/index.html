HOW TO PLAY

  • DROPTap, click or press Space to drop a cow onto the tower.
  • GLUECows that land softly stick together. Hard landings bounce them off.
  • HEARTSThree hearts. Lose a cow off the tower and you lose one.
  • WINDGusts push the tower. The flag and the meter show which way.
  • THE MOONReach 30 m to get to the Moon. Then keep stacking if you dare.
  • WEIRD LOOKSStack cows across all your runs to unlock stranger herd members.
