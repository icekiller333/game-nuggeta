HOW IT WORKS

  • Any item can arrive Rare, Mythic, Legendary — or very rarely Shiny. Same inspection, but every find is logged in your Collection.
  • New item types arrive as the days go on — Days 2, 3 and 4 each bring one.
  • Three strikes and you’re fired.
  • Stamp an item with a bug still on it — that’s a strike.
  • Beetles crawl. Camos blend in — watch for legs and shimmer. Mites are tiny and hide in seams.
  • Finish the day’s quota before the shift timer runs out.
  • Tap a bug to collect it.
  • Drag to spin the item and check every side.
