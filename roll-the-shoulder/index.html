Roll the shoulder

A strip of cement beside the road needs rolling. The strip is a little narrower than your machine, so you have only a few centimetres of room on each side, and it bends left and right.

Raise the lever to go, steer to keep the drum over the strip. Every rough patch you leave behind counts against you.

This 3D game needs WebGL, which didn’t start. Open the page in a current browser with graphics acceleration turned on.

Press Start rolling to begin.
