How to play

  • Drive the roller around the sidewalk hub. Park on a numbered pad and press the button to roll that shoulder. The blue ? pad replays the tutorial.
  • Steer with the left and right arrow keys (or A and D), or hold the on-screen buttons.
  • The travel lever sets speed: up is forward, the middle is neutral, down is reverse. Use the up and down arrow keys (or W and S), or drag it.
  • On a shoulder, keep the drum over the strip. Rough spots you miss count against you. Go a full machine width clear of it and the run restarts.
  • Each level has three stars: 90% rolled, 98% rolled, and finishing inside the time goal (45 seconds on most levels).
  • Stars unlock new levels. A striped barrier on the sidewalk shows how many you need.

This 3D game needs WebGL, which didn’t start. Open the page in a current browser with graphics acceleration turned on.
