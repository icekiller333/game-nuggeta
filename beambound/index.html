You went out for eels, as you have every night for twenty years. Dawn was due at six. It never came.

Instead the fog closed in — and beneath the hull, something vast turned over in the deep, as if waking.

Then, far ahead: a light. No lighthouse has ever stood on this coast.

You don't know who lit it, or why it moves. You only know the dark behind you has begun to count — and the light is the one place it cannot reach.
