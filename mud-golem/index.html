The pit behind the hill

You live by a swamp the town over the hill pretends not to smell. They take your firewood, your frogs, your quiet — and never share a thing back.

But the mud here is old. The mud remembers being walked on. Scoop enough of it together, dry it just right… and it might just walk back.

How to raise a golem

Dig mud. Shape balls. When a drying ball GLOWS, tap it to stack it — stack 3 and sing the pentagon rite. The golem marches off and pillages on its own — you keep digging while it works, and its mana arrives when it falls.

Stronger golems need more balls. Weaker ones are cheap. You choose, every time.
